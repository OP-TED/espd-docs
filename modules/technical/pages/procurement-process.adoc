== The Procurement Process

This page outlines where the ESPD fits within the EU public procurement lifecycle.

=== Procurement phases

The three main phases of the public procurement process are:

[arabic]
. *Pre-tendering* â€” preparation and planning of the procedure
. *Tendering* â€” publication, submission, qualification, evaluation, and award
. *Post-tendering* â€” contract implementation

The ESPD is specifically concerned with the *qualification* step within the tendering phase.

=== Qualification workflow

[arabic]
. Buyer publishes Contract Notice on TED + ESPD Request
. Economic Operator retrieves the ESPD Request
. Economic Operator fills in the ESPD Response (self-declarations + evidence references)
. Economic Operator submits ESPD Response as part of the tender
. Buyer evaluates ESPD Responses during qualification
. Only the winning tenderer provides full documentary evidence before award

=== Above and below the threshold

Article 4 of Directive 2014/24/EU defines value thresholds. Procedures above the threshold must publish on TED and follow specific rules. The ESPD Service supports both above- and below-threshold procedures.
