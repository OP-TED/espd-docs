== What is the ESPD?

The European Single Procurement Document (ESPD) is a self-declaration by an economic operator, used as preliminary evidence that it is not in any of the situations giving rise to exclusion and that it meets the selection criteria set by the buyer for a specific procurement. It replaces, at the tendering stage, the certificates and documents normally issued by public authorities or third parties.

[NOTE]
====
In the legal sense (Article 59 of Directive 2014/24/EU; Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/7, Annex 1) and in the *eProcurement Ontology (ePO)*, "the ESPD" is the *economic operator's self-declaration* — that is, what the economic operator submits. In ESPD-EDM terms this self-declaration is carried by the *ESPD Response*.

The *ESPD Request* is the buyer's expression of the exclusion grounds and selection criteria. It is not, in itself, "an ESPD"; it is the specification that the economic operator responds to.
====

=== ESPD and the ESPD-EDM documents

The *ESPD-EDM* (the technical exchange model) defines two electronic (XML) documents used to exchange this information:

* *ESPD Request* — prepared by the buyer (Contracting Authority). It expresses the exclusion grounds and selection criteria for the procurement. It enables the economic operator to produce its ESPD, but is not itself the ESPD.
* *ESPD Response* — prepared by the economic operator (offering party). It carries the actual ESPD self-declaration: the answers to each exclusion ground and selection criterion, together with references to the evidence.

TIP: Strictly, "the ESPD" is the economic operator's self-declaration (the Response). "ESPD Request" and "ESPD Response" are the two documents defined by the ESPD-EDM to exchange the qualification data electronically. The ESPD-EDM guide sometimes refers to both loosely as "ESPD documents".

=== Role in pre-qualification

The ESPD replaces the need for economic operators to produce large volumes of physical certificates and documentation at the tendering stage. Instead, operators provide self-declarations via the ESPD, and only the winning tenderer is required to supply full documentary evidence before contract award.

=== Key benefits

* Reduces administrative burden for both buyers and suppliers
* Facilitates cross-border participation in public procurement
* Supports small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs)
* Standardises the pre-qualification process across EU Member States
* Enables electronic exchange of qualification data

=== Legal basis

The ESPD is introduced by *Article 59 of Directive 2014/24/EU* and further specified by *Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/7*, which establishes the standard form: Annex 1 contains the instructions for its use, and Annex 2 contains the standard form itself. See xref:legal-framework.adoc[Legal Framework] for details.

=== The ESPD-EDM

The ESPD Exchange Data Model (ESPD-EDM) is the technical specification that defines how ESPD documents are structured, exchanged, and validated. It is based on UBL 2.4 and maintained by the Publications Office of the European Union.

* Current version: v5.0.0
* Repository: https://github.com/OP-TED/ESPD-EDM[OP-TED/ESPD-EDM]

==== Version history

The first version of the digital ESPD was released in 2016 (version 1.0.1). Since then, a number of new developments and enhancements have been made to the ESPD Service, taking into account Member States' requirements and needs in compliance with the Directive. The evolution of the ESPD has been developed in alignment with https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/public-procurement/digital-procurement/eforms_en[eForms].
