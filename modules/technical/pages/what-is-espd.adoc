== What is the ESPD?

The European Single Procurement Document (ESPD) is a self-declaration form used in EU public procurement as preliminary evidence that an economic operator meets the requirements to participate in a procurement procedure.

=== Two-part structure

The ESPD consists of two XML documents:

* *ESPD Request* — prepared by the buyer (Contracting Authority). It defines the exclusion grounds and selection criteria that economic operators must satisfy.
* *ESPD Response* — prepared by the economic operator (offering party). It contains the self-declarations and evidence references responding to the criteria defined in the Request.

=== Role in pre-qualification

The ESPD replaces the need for economic operators to produce large volumes of physical certificates and documentation at the tendering stage. Instead, operators provide self-declarations via the ESPD, and only the winning tenderer is required to supply full documentary evidence before contract award.

=== Key benefits

* Reduces administrative burden for both buyers and suppliers
* Facilitates cross-border participation in public procurement
* Supports small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs)
* Standardises the pre-qualification process across EU Member States
* Enables electronic exchange of qualification data

=== Legal basis

The ESPD is introduced by *Article 59 of Directive 2014/24/EU* and further specified by *Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/7*. See xref:legal-framework.adoc[Legal Framework] for details.

=== The ESPD-EDM

The ESPD Exchange Data Model (ESPD-EDM) is the technical specification that defines how ESPD documents are structured, exchanged, and validated. It is based on UBL 2.4 and maintained by the Publications Office of the European Union.

* Current version: v5.0.0
* Repository: https://github.com/OP-TED/ESPD-EDM[OP-TED/ESPD-EDM]
