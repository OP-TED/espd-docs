== Legal Framework

This page summarises the EU legislation that forms the legal basis for the ESPD.

=== Primary legislation

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Legislation |Relevance to ESPD

|link:https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A32014L0024[Directive 2014/24/EU]
|Public Procurement Directive. *Article 59* introduces the ESPD concept. *Article 57* defines exclusion grounds. *Article 58* defines selection criteria. *Article 4* defines thresholds.

|link:https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A32014L0025[Directive 2014/25/EU]
|Utilities Directive. Extends public procurement rules to the water, energy, transport and postal services sectors. Provides the legal basis for the ESPD in these sectors via *Article 80(3)*.

|link:https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A32014L0023[Directive 2014/23/EU]
|Concessions Directive. The ESPD is *not mandated* for concessions; Member States may allow its use for concession contracts (per Regulation (EU) 2016/7, Annex 1).

|link:https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0007[Regulation (EU) 2016/7]
|Implementing Regulation establishing the standard form for the European Single Procurement Document. Adopted under Article 59(2) of Directive 2014/24/EU and Article 80(3) of Directive 2014/25/EU.

|===

=== eForms alignment

The ESPD-EDM v5.0.0 is aligned with the eForms notification standard:

* Lot identification format (`LOT-XXXX`, `GLO-XXXX`)
* Shared code lists via EU Vocabularies
* Contract Notice reference linkage
* Procedure code consistency

More information: https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/public-procurement/digital-procurement/eforms_en[eForms at the European Commission]

=== Key legal requirements implemented in ESPD-EDM

* Mandatory exclusion grounds (Art. 57(1) and (2)) cannot be removed by the buyer
* Discretionary exclusion grounds (Art. 57(4)) can be selected/deselected
* Selection criteria must be linked to specific lots
* Each economic operator must submit its own ESPD
* Only the winning tenderer provides full evidence before award
* The ESPD must reference the Contract Notice published on TED (above threshold)

=== e-Certis

link:https://ec.europa.eu/tools/ecertis/[e-Certis] is the EU registry of criteria, evidences, and their structure. The ESPD-EDM validates criterion UUIDs and structures against e-Certis reference data.

=== UBL 2.4

The ESPD-EDM is based on link:https://docs.oasis-open.org/ubl/os-UBL-2.4/UBL-2.4.html[OASIS UBL 2.4]. The adoption of UBL is supported by link:https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32014D0771[Commission Decision 2014/771/EU] identifying UBL for referencing in public procurement.
