== The Procurement Process

This page outlines where the ESPD fits within the EU public procurement lifecycle.

The ESPD is used within procurement procedures governed by *Directive 2014/24/EU* (Article 59) and *Implementing Regulation (EU) 2016/7*. For the legal basis in detail, see xref:legal-framework.adoc[Legal Framework].

=== Procurement lifecycle phases

A public procurement procedure runs through a sequence of phases — *Planning*, *Competition Access*, *Submission*, *Evaluation*, *Award* and *Contract* (through to completion). The ESPD is concerned with the qualification of economic operators, which takes place around the *Competition Access*, *Submission* and *Evaluation* phases.

The qualification steps, in order:

[arabic]
. Buyer publishes the Contract Notice on TED, and may publish an ESPD Request alongside it
. Economic Operator retrieves the ESPD Request (if published; otherwise the criteria are in the procurement documents)
. Economic Operator fills in the ESPD Response (self-declarations + evidence references)
. Economic Operator submits the ESPD Response as part of the tender
. Buyer evaluates the ESPD Responses during qualification
. Before awarding the contract, the buyer requires the winning tenderer to submit full documentary evidence

NOTE: Under Article 59(4) of Directive 2014/24/EU, in addition to requiring evidence from the winner before award, the buyer may ask any tenderer or candidate to submit all or part of the supporting documents at any moment during the procedure, where this is needed to ensure the procedure is conducted properly.

For the full lifecycle, including the diagram showing where the ESPD Request and ESPD Response fit across these phases, see xref:what-is-espd.adoc[What is the ESPD?].

=== Above and below the threshold

Article 4 of Directive 2014/24/EU defines value thresholds. Procedures above the threshold must publish on TED and follow specific rules. The ESPD Service supports both above- and below-threshold procedures.
