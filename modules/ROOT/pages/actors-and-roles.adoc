== Actors and Roles

This page defines the actors involved in the ESPD exchange and their roles.

=== Buyer

The organisation that initiates the procurement procedure and publishes the ESPD Request. The most common type of buyer is a *contracting authority* — defined in Article 2 of Directive 2014/24/EU as a state, regional or local authority, body governed by public law, or association formed by such authorities.

*Responsibilities in ESPD:*

* Defines exclusion grounds and selection criteria
* Publishes the ESPD Request with the Contract Notice
* Evaluates ESPD Responses
* Requests full evidence from the winning tenderer (and may request supporting documents from any tenderer or candidate at any moment during the procedure)

=== Economic Operator (EO)

Any natural or legal person or public entity that offers the execution of works, supply of products, or provision of services on the market. Each economic operator (hereafter also *EO*) participating in a tender must provide its own ESPD Response.

*Roles in the ESPD-EDM:*

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Role |Description

|Sole Tenderer
|Single economic operator participating alone.

|Group Leader
|Leader of a Consortium, Joint Venture or other group. Point of contact with the buyer.

|Group Member (not leader)
|Member of a group that supplies a percentage of the contract.

|Other Entity (relied upon)
|Entity upon which the main contractor relies to meet selection criteria.

|Subcontractor
|Entity upon which the main contractor does not rely to meet selection criteria.
|===

The role is specified via `cac:EconomicOperatorRole/cbc:RoleCode` using the link:{url-tree}/codelists/EoRoleType.gc[EoRoleType] code list.

=== Service Provider

An optional actor that provides the ESPD Service platform used by both buyer and economic operator to create and manage ESPD documents. Service Provider data is optional in the ESPD documents.

=== Key rules per role

* *One ESPD Response per economic operator* — each participant submits their own
* *One ESPD Response per lot* — each EO submits a separate response per lot they tender for
* *Group Leader's responsibility* — collects all group members' ESPDs and submits with the tender
* *Subcontractors* do not need to respond to selection criteria
