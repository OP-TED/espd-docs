== Preparing the ESPD Request

Buyers (Contracting Authorities) use the ESPD Request to define the exclusion grounds and selection criteria that Economic Operators (offering parties) must satisfy in order to participate in a procurement procedure.

_The ESPD process from the buyer perspective_

The buyer prepares the ESPD Request as part of the procurement procedure documentation. When published, the ESPD Request is made available alongside the Contract Notice and is linked to it via the notice publication reference number. If it is not published, the exclusion grounds and selection criteria are stated in the procurement documents (specifications) instead.

=== Key responsibilities

As a buyer you are responsible for:

* Defining which exclusion grounds apply to the procedure (mandatory grounds from Article 57(1) and (2) are pre-selected; discretionary grounds from Article 57(4) can be added or removed);
* Selecting the applicable selection criteria (suitability, economic and financial standing, technical and professional ability, quality assurance);
* Specifying any requirements or thresholds for selection criteria (e.g. minimum turnover, number of years of experience);
* Associating selection criteria with the relevant lots (exclusion grounds apply to all lots automatically);
* Making the exclusion grounds and selection criteria available to economic operators — by publishing the ESPD Request together with the Contract Notice, or by stating them in the procurement documents (specifications).

The buyer reviews the exclusion grounds. Grounds relating to criminal convictions (Article 57(1)) and payment of taxes or social security (Article 57(2)) are mandatory and pre-selected:

=== Lots and criteria

Important considerations when working with lots:

* Exclusion grounds apply automatically to *all lots* in the procedure.
* Selection criteria apply *per lot* and may differ between lots.
* Each Economic Operator must submit a separate ESPD Response per lot or group of lots they tender for.

=== Evaluating responses

After receiving ESPD Responses, the buyer reviews the responses and supporting evidence provided by Economic Operators. The "Overview" option allows reviewing all information from a single page:

image::form/of28.png[]

_Options available after completing the ESPD form_

=== Reuse of ESPD Requests

It is not necessary to create an ESPD Request from scratch for each procedure. ESPD documents can be downloaded, edited, and reused in further procurement procedures, making the process more efficient.
