== Completing the ESPD Response

Economic operators use the ESPD Response to demonstrate that they are eligible and capable of participating in a procurement procedure by responding to the exclusion grounds and selection criteria set by the buyer in the ESPD Request.

For where the ESPD Request and ESPD Response fit within the procurement lifecycle, see xref:ROOT:procurement-process.adoc[The Procurement Process].

The economic operator retrieves the ESPD Request, if one is published alongside the Contract Notice, provides its ESPD Response on an ESPD Service platform, and submits it as part of its tender.

NOTE: If no ESPD Request is published, the exclusion grounds and selection criteria are stated only in the procurement documents (specifications), and the economic operator prepares its ESPD Response based on those.

=== Roles of the economic operator

An economic operator can participate in a procurement procedure in different roles:

* *Sole Tenderer:* sole economic operator participating by itself;
* *Group Leader:* the leader of a Consortium, Joint Venture or other type of group. The Group Leader is the point of contact with the buyer and speaks on behalf of the group;
* *Group Member (not leader):* a group member that supplies a percentage of the contract;
* *Other entity (relied upon):* an entity upon which the main contractor, the group, or another subcontractor relies in order to meet the selection criteria;
* *Subcontractor:* an entity upon which the main contractor, the group, or another subcontractor does not rely in order to meet the selection criteria, and which is not a group member.

NOTE: Each participating party provides and signs its own ESPD Response. It is the Group Leader's responsibility to collect the completed ESPD Response documents from all parties and submit them in the tender.

=== Lots

Important considerations for economic operators regarding lots:

* Each economic operator submits a *separate ESPD Response per lot* (or group of lots) they tender for.
* Different parties might have different roles in different lots (e.g. a relied-on entity in some lots, a subcontractor in others).
* The exclusion grounds apply to all lots; selection criteria may differ per lot.

=== Reuse of ESPD Responses

It is not necessary to create an ESPD Response from scratch for each procedure. A completed ESPD Response can be downloaded, saved locally, and uploaded in subsequent procedures. Only the information that differs from the previous procedure needs to be changed, making the process more efficient over time.
