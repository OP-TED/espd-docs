== What is an ESPD Service?

An ESPD Service is an implementation of the ESPD-EDM model that provides an online platform where public buyers and economic operators can create, edit, export and import the ESPD Request and ESPD Response as XML documents. It enforces compliance with EU procurement legislation and streamlines the qualification process, enabling participation in public procurement by different types of economic operators.

An ESPD Service also validates the documents it produces, so that the exported ESPD Request and ESPD Response conform to the ESPD-EDM rules before they are used in a procedure.

== Available ESPD Services

There are a number of ESPD Services available, provided by EU Member States or commercial service providers. An updated list of ESPD Service providers can be found at the https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/single-market/public-procurement/digital-procurement/european-single-procurement-document-and-ecertis_en[European Commission's ESPD page].

NOTE: Currently, the European Commission does not maintain a centralised ESPD Service. It is up to Member States to implement the latest ESPD version available.

== How to use an ESPD Service

=== For buyers

[arabic]
. Access an ESPD Service platform provided by your Member State or a commercial provider.
. Create an ESPD Request by defining the exclusion grounds and selection criteria for your procurement procedure.
. Link the ESPD Request to your Contract Notice using the eForms notice publication reference number.
. Publish the ESPD Request alongside your Contract Notice.
. After the tender deadline, review the ESPD Responses submitted by economic operators.

For detailed guidance on what to provide, see xref:for-buyers.adoc[For Buyers].

=== For economic operators

[arabic]
. Find the procurement procedure you wish to participate in (if published, the ESPD Request is available alongside the Contract Notice; otherwise the criteria are in the procurement documents).
. Access the ESPD Service platform indicated by the buyer.
. Fill in the ESPD Response, answering the exclusion grounds and selection criteria defined by the buyer.
. Provide references to evidence supporting your responses.
. Download and submit the completed ESPD Response as part of your tender.

For detailed guidance on what to provide, see xref:for-economic-operators.adoc[For Economic Operators].

== Starting from scratch or reusing a previous ESPD

You have two options when using an ESPD Service:

* *From scratch:* fill in all information for the first time. Once completed, the document can be saved locally for future reuse.
* *Upload and edit:* upload a previously saved ESPD XML document and only change the details that differ from the previous procedure. This applies to both buyers and economic operators.

This makes the process significantly more efficient over time, as most information (company identity, standard criteria responses, etc.) remains the same across procedures.

== Linking to an eForms notice

The information in the ESPD Service is linked to an eForms notice by the publication reference number generated when a buyer publishes their Contract Notice on TED. The buyer prepares the ESPD Request as a subsequent step in the procurement process, and economic operators find the relevant ESPD Request by searching the tender notices.
