== Lots in Public Procurement

A procurement procedure may be divided into *lots* — separate portions of the contract that economic operators can tender for individually. Lots allow buyers to split large contracts into smaller parts, making them accessible to a wider range of suppliers, including small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).

The ESPD supports multi-lot procedures through a structured approach that distinguishes how exclusion grounds and selection criteria are applied across lots.

=== What are lots?

A lot represents a distinct portion of a procurement procedure. For example, a buyer procuring IT services might divide the contract into:

* *LOT-0001* — Software development
* *LOT-0002* — Infrastructure maintenance
* *LOT-0003* — Help desk support

Each lot may have its own technical requirements and selection criteria, while exclusion grounds apply uniformly to all lots.

Lot identifiers follow the eForms format: `LOT-0001`, `LOT-0002`, etc. Groups of lots use the prefix `GLO-` (e.g. `GLO-0001`).

=== How lots affect the ESPD

==== For buyers

When preparing the ESPD Request:

* There is *one ESPD Request per procedure*, regardless of how many lots the procedure contains.
* *Exclusion grounds* are defined once and apply to all lots — no lot-specific configuration is needed.
* *Selection criteria* are linked to specific lots. Different lots may require different qualification criteria. For example, Lot 1 might require a minimum annual turnover while Lot 2 requires specific technical certifications.

==== For offering parties

When completing the ESPD Response:

* The economic operator submits *one ESPD Response per lot* they wish to tender for.
* Each response must address *all exclusion grounds* (since they apply to all lots).
* Each response must address only the *selection criteria applicable to that specific lot*.
* If an economic operator wants to participate in 3 lots, they must submit 3 separate ESPD Responses.

=== Roles within a lot

Each participating economic operator must fill in their own ESPD Response for each lot. Group members (in a consortium or joint venture) each submit a separate ESPD, indicating their role:

* Sole tenderer
* Group leader
* Group member
* Subcontractor
* Other entity relied upon

See xref:for-economic-operators.adoc[For Economic Operators] for details on group participation.
